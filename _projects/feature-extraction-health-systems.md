## My contribution

- Planning the teaching flow and instructional material.

## What I value about this work

Technical knowledge is only useful when the learner can turn it into a mental model and then into action. I treat task design as an engineering problem: make the objective clear, choose the right level of challenge, and give students enough structure to reason independently.
